First on the roster

The GDVO

The Guy Daniel Vastola Organization. Guy Daniel writes it, produces it, and cuts it in his own room on Route 33 — the same room where he spent thirty years engineering other people’s records before he made his own.

The debut single “Peace” pairs him with The Chi-Lites, the Chicago group behind eleven Top Ten R&B hits, and the late Jamaican reggae pioneer Denroy Morgan. It was tracked between City Lights in New Jersey and Chicago, and released to launch a global peace campaign on 20 January 2021.

Tip the artist

Money that goes to the player, not the platform

Tip any artist from any page, any amount. Tips pass through to the artist in full — Tune2This takes nothing but the card fee. Every dollar moves you up their board.

The reason it is a board and not a button: on the platforms where tipping actually works, it is competitive. Tencent Music earns roughly seventy per cent of its revenue from tips, gifts and donations rather than subscriptions, because fans can see where they stand.
