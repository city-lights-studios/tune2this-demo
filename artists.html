Why five slots are empty The brief calls for roughly twelve featured artists on the home page. None of that exists yet: no roster names, no artist photos, no bios, no audio. The photographs behind the empty slots are City Lights session shots standing in as texture — the people in them are not identified and are not being presented as roster artists. Filling this grid is the single biggest blocker between this demo and a launch.
A note on the studio’s client list City Lights has recorded some very well-known names over thirty-five years. Those are studio credits and they live on the studio page, presented as the studio’s own claims. They are not Tune2This artists and must never appear on this roster page — a label implying it represents Cher or Bruce Springsteen is a problem no disclaimer fixes.
